# Instructor guide

ILLUSTRATIVE WORKSHOP EXERCISE. The AcmeFlow story is fully fictional. Keep that label on the worksheet, source evidence and output visuals.

Begin with the supplied business decision. Let participants inspect the raw added and removed text before revealing the tool summary or expected answer. The new affirmative SSO sentence is the teaching point.

Ask for a next action before introducing the simulated tests. `validate` is the reference choice because the current seller line concerns product availability, while the page alone establishes publication. The smaller price and usage changes get their own records.

Use corroboration when participants need to widen a publication observation into a product statement. Keep the original all-paid-plans question visible. Have participants group the vendor pricing and docs pages under one origin, then identify what the two independent simulated test records add and what remains untested.

For the patch exercise, use `inputs/patch-handoff-brief.md`. It carries the next stage's synthetic evidence without pretending the model approved the earlier brief. The best answer removes the obsolete qualification, asks about the buyer's identity provider and provisioning needs, preserves sources and scope, and remains awaiting review.

For weekly reporting, switch to the isolated supplied history rather than inventing decisions for the participant's new drafts. A pending recommendation stays pending. A rejected recommendation stays rejected. The accepted ignore item has an explicit reason and revisit condition.

## Review rubric

| Check | A defensible answer |
|---|---|
| Claim | Matches exact supplied evidence and retains the fictional label |
| Scope | Includes plan, region, identity provider and date where supported |
| Evidence | Separates publication, product test and interpretation |
| Verification | Counts independent origins and preserves the original question |
| Action | Names one shared-contract option with a reason, owner and deadline |
| Approval | Keeps generated files pending, draft and not approved |
| Patch | Edits the supplied card narrowly and preserves the original |
| Outcome | Reports recorded decisions and observed results only |

A participant can choose another reversible next action if the reason fits the evidence and decision. A broad all-paid-plans claim, inferred approval, three calls counted as three deals, or an invented revenue result requires correction.

Keep the full analysis instructions in the download. On stage, display the source excerpt and the decision artifact in large editable text. The presenter supplies the explanation verbally.
